Introduction

Purpose
This document defines the agreed functional and non-functional scope for the DAVKAWT Alumni Portal — a web platform that enables alumni of DAV Khagaul to register, connect, and engage with the Trust. It forms the basis of the engagement between Creative Mark (Vendor) and DAVKAWT (Client) and is the reference for design, development, and acceptance.

Scope
The portal will provide:
Alumni self-registration with admin approval.
A searchable, role-restricted alumni directory.
Alumni profile pages with privacy controls.
Announcements, events calendar, and a discussion forum.
Membership fee collection via an integrated payment gateway.
Automated email notifications.
An admin dashboard for moderation, payments, exports, and analytics.
Responsive, mobile-first design.

Product Perspective
A new, standalone web application that will be the central digital presence of DAVKAWT for alumni engagement. It integrates with a payment gateway, an email service, and a cloud database, all selected for reliability and Indian data compliance.

Operating Environment
End-user devices: Modern desktop and mobile browsers (current and previous major versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox).
Hosting: Cloud platform with Indian region preference for data residency.

The payment gateway shall be Easebuzz, selected for competitive transaction rates and full coverage of UPI, cards, netbanking, and wallets.

Functional Requirements

This section lists the functional capabilities of the portal. Each capability shall be delivered to the standard of a typical, modern alumni platform; specific implementation details are at the Vendor's discretion.

Registration & Approval
Public registration form capturing name, batch / year, course, contact details, current location, occupation, and other relevant fields.
Email OTP verification before submission.

Admin review queue with approve / reject actions and optional remarks.
Email notification to the user on approval or rejection.

Authentication & Roles
Email + password login; optional Google sign-in/LinkedIn
Password reset via secure email link.
Four role tiers (Section 2.2) with access restricted accordingly.
Session management with automatic expiry and secure logout.

Alumni Directory
Listing of verified alumni with, name, batch, course, and city /Photo.
Search by name, company, or keyword.
Filters by batch / year, course, city, state, country, and industry.
Pagination and sort options.
Directory access restricted to verified alumni and administrators.

Alumni Profiles
Detailed alumni profile page showing professional and educational information.
Profile edit by the owning alumnus; selected fields editable by administrators.
Privacy controls — alumni can hide email or phone from the directory.

Announcements
Admin-published announcements visible to all verified alumni.
Rich-text content with images.
Pin and schedule options.

Events
Admin-created events with title, date and time, venue or online link, description, and banner.
Event reminders via email.
Past events archive.

Discussion Forum
Threaded discussion categories created by admins.
Posting, replying, and basic moderation by admins (edit, delete, lock).
Read and write access restricted to verified alumni.

Notifications & Email
Transactional emails for: account creation, approval / rejection, password reset, payment confirmation and receipt, event reminders, membership renewal reminders.
Branded email templates approved by DAVKAWT before go-live.
Bulk announcement email by administrators to selected segments (all alumni, batch, course, etc.).

Membership & Payments
Configurable membership plans (e.g., one-time registration fee and / or annual membership).
Online payment via Easebuzz supporting UPI, credit / debit cards, netbanking, wallets, and other modes enabled on the merchant account.
Payment confirmation page and emailed PDF receipt.
Retry option for failed or abandoned payments.
Annual membership tracks an expiry date; reminder emails before expiry.
Admin-initiated refund through the payment gateway dashboard.

Admin Dashboard
Summary metrics: total alumni, pending approvals, paid members, upcoming events.
Approval queue, alumni management (search, edit, deactivate), event management, announcement management, forum moderation.
Payment record listing with filters by date, status, and plan.
Configuration of membership plans, email templates, and basic site settings.

Reports & Exports
CSV / Excel export of alumni list, payment records, and event RSVPs based on selected filters.
Basic engagement analytics (registrations over time, active users, popular content).

Data Handling & Security

All traffic between the user's browser and the platform shall be encrypted using TLS.
Passwords shall be stored using industry-standard hashing.
Role-based access controls shall be enforced at both the application and database layers.
Administrative actions on alumni data shall be audit-logged.
Data ownership: all alumni and operational data is owned by DAVKAWT. The Vendor acts strictly as a data processor for the duration of the engagement.

Commercial & Engagement Terms

These commercial and engagement terms form an integral part of this SRS. By countersigning this document, DAVKAWT acknowledges and accepts these terms.

Scope & Change Management
The scope is defined exclusively by this SRS. Any addition or material change after sign-off shall be raised in writing as a Change Request and shall be estimated and approved separately before work commences.

Payment Terms
50% of the project fee payable on SRS sign-off, before development begins.
50% payable on completion of UAT and prior to final handover.
Domain, hosting, email-sending, and payment gateway transaction charges are borne directly by DAVKAWT.
Production credentials, and final documentation will be transferred to DAVKAWT after full payment is received.
Invoices unpaid beyond 30 days may attract suspension of services until settled.

Intellectual Property
On full and final payment, the custom application code developed specifically for DAVKAWT is assigned to DAVKAWT. Open-source frameworks and libraries used remain governed by their respective licences. The Vendor retains its prior knowhow, reusable internal utilities, and methodology.

Confidentiality & Data Ownership
All alumni data, content, and operational records are owned by DAVKAWT. The Vendor and its personnel will treat all such data as strictly confidential during and after the engagement and will use it solely for the purpose of delivering and supporting the platform.

Warranty
The Vendor warrants that the delivered system will materially conform to this SRS for a period of 30 days from go-live. Defects reported in writing during this period that are reproducible deviations from this SRS will be corrected at no additional cost. The warranty does not cover feature changes, third-party service changes, or issues caused by client-side modifications.

Third-Party Services
The platform depends on third-party services (payment gateway, hosting, email, cloud database, etc.). Service availability, Fee, policy changes, and KYC processes of these providers are outside the Vendor's control. Adapting the platform to material future changes by these providers shall be treated as a Change Request.

Limitation of Liability
The Vendor's aggregate liability under this engagement, regardless of cause, shall not exceed the total fees actually received from DAVKAWT for this project. The Vendor shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages including loss of profit, loss of data, or business interruption.

Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)
Year 1 AMC is included as per the commercial proposal and covers routine bug fixes, basic monitoring, and minor adjustments. Year 2 onwards AMC is offered at ₹20,000 per annum, payable in advance, and excludes new features, redesigns, and major upgrades, which shall be treated as Change Requests. AMC must be renewed in writing before expiry.

Termination
Either party may terminate this engagement with 30 days' written notice. On termination, DAVKAWT shall pay for all work completed up to that date; the Vendor shall hand over completed work artefacts subject to payment clearance. Confidentiality obligations survive termination.

Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events outside reasonable control, including natural disasters, pandemics, government actions, internet or cloud provider outages, or labour disputes.

Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
This engagement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation; failing resolution, through arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, conducted in English at a venue mutually agreed by the parties.

Communication
DAVKAWT shall nominate a single SPoC authorised to approve scope, sign deliverables, and process payments. Standard working days are Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00 IST, excluding Indian public holidays.

Acknowledgement
By countersigning this SRS, the authorised signatory of DAVKAWT confirms agreement to both the technical scope (Sections 1–9) and the commercial and engagement terms (Section 10).
